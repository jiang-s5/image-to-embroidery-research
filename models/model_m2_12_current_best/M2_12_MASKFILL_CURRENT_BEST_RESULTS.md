# M2.12 Mask-Fill Candidate-Augmented Current Best Results

Date: 2026-06-22

M2.12 extends M2.10 by adding an experimental dense mask-fill candidate:

`mask_fill_rows16_c6`

The candidate is generated by `tools/generate_mask_fill_dst.py` and evaluated by `tools/run_mask_fill_eval.py`. It uses connected components plus horizontal serpentine fill rows, then allows only short mask-safe row connections.

This is not yet a professional fill/satin digitizer. It is a diagnostic candidate for testing whether dense coverage can be introduced without breaking executable DST quality.

## Mask-Fill Diagnostic Result

The mask-fill candidate itself produced nearly complete coverage but poor executability:

| Candidate | Hard Fail | Mean Unified Loss | Mean Jump Count | Mean Trim Count | Mean Coverage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| mask_fill_rows12_c6 | 30/33 | 0.333721 | 239.939394 | 35.727273 | 0.999998 |
| mask_fill_rows16_c6 | 31/33 | 0.309077 | 148.060606 | 21.787879 | 0.999656 |
| mask_fill_rows12_c10 | 30/33 | 0.332807 | 233.515152 | 35.727273 | 0.999998 |

Relaxed row-connection variants reduced jumps slightly but increased off-mask and visible connector risk:

| Candidate | Hard Fail | Mean Unified Loss | Mean Jump Count | Mean Off-Mask mm | Mean Visible Connectors |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| rows16_c12_in050 | 32/33 | 0.441134 | 138.272727 | 10.003442 | 2.151515 |
| rows16_c12_in025 | 32/33 | 0.457177 | 137.424242 | 15.054524 | 3.939394 |
| rows20_c12_in050 | 31/33 | 0.424977 | 116.545455 | 8.319227 | 2.151515 |

This shows that naive dense fill is not enough. It gives the visual impression of fill, but it does not yet behave like real digitizing software because it lacks segment-level fill regions, satin/border planning, underlay, and color-layer ordering.

## Interpretation

M2.12 is a safe current-best package rather than a large realism breakthrough.

It improves public leave-one-out executable quality, keeps zero hard_fail, and does not regress core-to-full or incoming review evaluation. The new mask-fill branch also gives a clear research result: dense row fill must be made segment-aware before it can deliver professional embroidery-like output.

## Next Real Optimization

The next model should not simply add more global fill rows. It should introduce segment-level fill/satin planning:

- split foreground into fill regions, satin borders, and running-stitch details;
- generate border/outline stitches before or after fill depending on layer;
- use component-level entry/exit points instead of row-level jumps;
- add underlay or edge-walk connectors to hide row transitions;
- evaluate both executable quality and render-back realism.

M2.12 remains the strongest current safe pipeline, but professional stitch realism is still the main unsolved gap.
